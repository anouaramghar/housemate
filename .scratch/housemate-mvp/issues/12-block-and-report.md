# 12 — Block and report

**What to build:** A student can block someone so they can never reach them again, and report someone
so that behaviour has consequences beyond one person's block. Reporting is reachable from **every**
surface where a person is encountered — nobody should have to hunt for it in a moment that matters.

After reporting, the student is told what happens next. Reporting into a void is worse than no
reporting at all, because it spends the user's courage and returns nothing.

No moderation team exists. Reports are recorded and queued for a human rather than actioned
automatically (decision ticket 09 is still open). Say so honestly in the confirmation rather than
implying an enforcement that will not happen.

**Blocked by:** 07 — Express interest, match, and unlock chat.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Blocking is immediate and mutual in effect: neither party can reach the other afterwards
- [ ] A blocked person disappears from browse, from match candidacy, and from chat
- [ ] Blocking never notifies the blocked person
- [ ] Reporting is reachable from the browse card, the profile, the chat and the Household member list
- [ ] A report captures the reported Person, the reporter, a reason and free-text context
- [ ] Reports land in a queue readable by an operator, and are never auto-actioned
- [ ] After reporting, the student sees an honest description of what happens next
- [ ] Blocking and reporting are independent — one does not require the other
