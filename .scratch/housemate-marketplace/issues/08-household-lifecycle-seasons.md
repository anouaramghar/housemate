# Household lifecycle and season boundaries

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 01
Parent: [Student Housemate Marketplace](../map.md)

## Question

A household moves **forming → locked → coordinating** (constraint 12). What happens after that is
undefined, and so is what happens to households that never get there.

Decide:

- Does a household **end** when the lease is signed, or does it persist through the tenancy as the
  object reputation attaches to? Constraint 14 — off-season reviewing — implies it persists.
- At the end of a housing season, do households **dissolve**? Do profiles reset? Does a household
  **roll over** if the same people renew?
- Are half-formed, never-locked households eventually **cleared out**? On what trigger, and how do
  their members find out?
- Can a person belong to more than one forming household at a time? Allowing it helps liquidity;
  forbidding it makes the lock mean more.

This determines what "the off-season" actually is in product terms, which the whole reputation moat
depends on.

Waits on [Can a household exist with no property?](01-household-without-property.md).
