# Safety when a match goes wrong

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 07
Parent: [Student Housemate Marketplace](../map.md)

## Question

Trust is the differentiator (constraint 7), so the failure path matters more here than in most
products. A single bad story on one campus is existential when your whole claim is safety.

Decide what exists for:

- **Blocking** someone, **reporting** someone, and getting a member **removed** from a household.
- What happens to a **locked** household when a member is reported or removed — does it reopen, does
  the lock survive, are the others notified and told why?
- **Who reviews reports?** There is no moderation team. Is it automated, community-driven, or does
  it simply queue until a human exists?
- Does the **university partner** have any role? A report about one of their students may create an
  obligation to tell them — and that cuts both ways for user trust.
- What happens to someone's reputation when they are removed **for cause**, given written reviews
  only unlock after a match (constraint 15)?

Waits on [Household consent model](07-household-consent-model.md) — removal thresholds are decided
there, and this ticket builds on them.
