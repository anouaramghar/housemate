# Compatibility dimensions — which traits, and what makes a match good

Type: prototype
Status: open
Blocked by: —
Parent: [Student Housemate Marketplace](../map.md)

## Question

Matching is hard filters → compatibility score → double opt-in. The filters and the score have no
content yet.

Build a rough, throwaway artifact via `/prototype` — a draft questionnaire plus a sketch of how
answers combine into a score — concrete enough to react to. Then decide:

- Which traits are **hard filters** (a mismatch removes someone from the pool entirely) versus
  **soft signals** (a mismatch only lowers the score)? Budget, area and move-in date are almost
  certainly hard; sleep schedule and cleanliness probably are not.
- How many questions before answering becomes a chore people abandon?
- Are traits **self-reported only**, or can housemate reputation feed back and correct them? Someone
  who calls themselves tidy and has three reviews saying otherwise is the interesting case.
- Is the score shown as a number, a band, or just an ordering?

Constraint from charting: lifestyle traits plus gender preference only. No other identity dimension
(map, constraint 10).

Link the prototype from this ticket rather than pasting it in.
