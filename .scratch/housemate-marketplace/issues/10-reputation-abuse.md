# Reputation abuse — fake and retaliatory reviews

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 05
Parent: [Student Housemate Marketplace](../map.md)

## Question

Housemates who fall out will write reviews about each other. The moat is worthless if it can be
gamed, and actively harmful if it becomes a weapon.

Decide:

- Can a review be **disputed**, and by what process?
- Is there a **right of reply** shown alongside the review?
- What stops **coordinated inflation** — friends joining a household briefly to review each other up?
- What stops a **retaliatory pile-on** — a group punishing the member who left, especially given
  breaking a lock is already recorded (constraint 16)?
- Are reviews **editable**, **permanent**, or do they **expire**? A bad review from first year
  following someone into their masters is a real fairness question.
- Does the **release** mechanic (constraint 16) also suppress reviews, or only the walked-away
  penalty?

Bear in mind the visibility rule from charting: the score is public but written reviews only unlock
after a match (constraint 15). That shrinks the blast radius of a bad review without removing it —
the score still moves, and the score is what people browse on.

Waits on [Reputation rules](05-reputation-rules.md), which decides who can review whom in the first
place.
